# IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking — submission format facts (fetched 2026-09-03)

Source: the ToN submissions page (sites.google.com/view/ieee-acm-ton/submissions), fetched this
session; the comsoc.org author page returned 404 and ton.lids.mit.edu refused the connection.
Web-search snippets from other mirrors quoted both "14 pages" and "16 pages" as the typical
maximum; the live ToN page says 16. Treat 16 as the ceiling and 14 as the comfortable target.

| item | rule |
|---|---|
| Format | two-column, single-spaced, 10-point font; IEEE Transactions style files (LaTeX `IEEEtran`, `\documentclass[journal]{IEEEtran}`) |
| Length | "typical maximum length of submitted papers is 16 pages in the two-column Transactions format"; longer needs EiC approval or supplementary material |
| Page charges | 10 pages free of charge for accepted papers; additional pages $220 per page (waiver within 30 days of acceptance) |
| Abstract | 150–250 words, one paragraph, no abbreviations, footnotes, references, displayed equations or tables; self-contained |
| Keywords | three or four keywords/phrases (rendered as *Index Terms* in IEEEtran) |
| Supplementary material | appendices are peer-reviewed and must be submitted with the initial manuscript; main body must be self-contained |
| Citations | IEEE numeric style, `[n]`, `\bibliographystyle{IEEEtran}` |
| Prior publication | conference precursor acceptable only if substantially expanded |
| Graphical abstract | optional |

Planning consequence for this manuscript: target 12–14 pages of body + references in IEEEtran
journal format (the 8 verified-ToN corpus papers run 14–15 pages in author-formatted arXiv
versions, some with appendices); abstract 200–240 words; 3–4 Index Terms; related work as a
standalone section; appendices only for material a reviewer needs but the body does not.
